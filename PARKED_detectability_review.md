## The `[detect]` section of the field control file configures nothing

Parked 24 Nov 2026. A defect, not a tuning question.

`fieldrun/common/fieldconfig.jl` requires, validates and prints six `[detect]`
keys -- `theta`, `min_thick_m`, `occam_chi2_max`, `doi_widthfrac`,
`doi_widthfrac_alt`, `metric` -- and no stage reads any of them (`grep` over
`fieldrun/` at `815e51d`: the only readers are `fieldconfig.jl` and its test).
`detect_aquifer.jl:54-58` hardcodes `THETA_MIN = 0.15`, `THICK_MIN = 10.0`,
`THETA_DETECT = 0.05` and `FLOOR_MAIN = 0.15`, while `config/udf_example.toml`
and the Birrindudu files declare `theta = 0.10` and `min_thick_m = 5.0`. Anyone
tuning these keys sees no effect and may conclude the data are insensitive.
Same class as the duplicated parameter surface already on record: the control
file and an inline hardcode both declare a setting, and the hardcode wins
silently. To do: read them in the stage, or remove them from the validator and
the example files. (`[detect] detectability` on `detectability-block` is the
one key the stage does read.)

## Figure 4's sensitivity-DOI line uses the cell-size-dependent rule

Parked 24 Nov 2026. A figure change, caused by a bug, not a re-tuning. Stage 3
uses no depth limit, so the paper's intervals and W values do not move.

`figures/fig04_snmr_posteriors.jl:34-41` `sensitivity_doi` is the per-cell rule:
`dmin = 1/sqrt(sum_q (w_i|K_iq|/sigma_q)^2)` for one cell alone, so `dmin` scales
with cell thickness and the depth moves with the grid. Evaluated on the figure's
own inputs -- `smr_data_murray{3P,2P}T.jld2` regenerated by `tools/smr_data.jl`
at `4ac7f7d`, typical tier -- against the coherent sensitivity DOI
(`sens_doi_coherent`, `detectability-block`, the section below each depth
perturbed together, summed over depth before squaring):

| column | fig04 rule, 1 m / 0.5 m cells | coherent, 1 m / 0.5 m cells |
|---|---|---|
| 3P (test one) | 56.6 / 56.6 m | 64.5 / 63.2 m |
| 2P (test two) | 51.7 / 45.9 m | 67.3 / 67.3 m |

Over the four v2/brackish columns and 4.6-400 nV the cell rule's half/current
ratio is 0.26-0.98 and it reaches 0 m by 50-200 nV; the coherent rule's is
0.97-1.04. Two things to settle with the change: the figure header quotes
59.2 m (test one) and 49.4 m (test two), which the figure code does not
reproduce on the regenerated inputs (stale text, or archived inputs that
differ -- not checked against the archive); and which rule replaces it (see
the next entry).

## Detectability: which perturbation defines the limit

Parked 24 Nov 2026, for a decision, not more computation.

Three grid-stable rules on `detectability-block` disagree by up to 15 m
(typical tier, 1 m cells):

| | blk10 | blkP | sens_doi_coherent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3P | 77.6 | 77.6 | 64.5 |
| 3S | 77.6 | 74.6 | 61.8 |
| 2Pv2 | 77.6 | 68.7 | 73.1 |
| 2Sv2 | 77.6 | 65.9 | 70.1 |
| Mulan31 (inferred noise) | 90.8 | 84.0 | 70.1 |
| Mulan32 (inferred noise) | 80.8 | 77.6 | 67.3 |

blk10 is a 10 m block, |sum K̃|; blkP is the same block projected onto the
response phase; sens_doi_coherent perturbs the whole section below the depth by
0.05, projected. The phase projection explains part of the blk10 excess
(0-12 m) but not the gap to the coherent DOI: on 3P it removes nothing, and on
the v2 columns blkP lands below sens_doi_coherent. The coherent C is monotone in
depth, so the crossing convention is not the cause either. The rules answer
different questions -- "is a 10 m layer at z visible" (block) against "is a
change of everything below z visible" (section, with cancellation between the
layer and what lies beneath) -- and the choice is which question the depth
limit should answer.

## SNMRForward overflows silently on fine conductivity stacks

Parked 24 Nov 2026.

`SNMRForward` returns finite, wrong kernels on thin-layer conductivity models:
800 x 0.25 m gave |kernel| ~1e55 and V0 ~1e62 nV, 200 x 1 m ~1e11 nV, while
2 m and 5 m layers agree (3P V0 356-2920 nV). Nothing raises. `tools/smr_data.jl`
records it in its header and works around it on the 65-layer AEM grid;
`fieldrun/test/detectability_cellsize.jl` hit it anyway and now refuses a
kernel whose V0 is outside 10 nV-100 uV. Anything else that builds a kernel on
a fine stack -- a per-draw kernel (C4), a refined truth -- gets garbage
silently. To do: a guard at the forward call (an amplitude bound, or a check
against a coarsened stack), not in each caller.
